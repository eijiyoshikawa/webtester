RENTAL GUIDE

Rental Guide

Booking & Use

Bookings are accepted up to 2 months in advance, in 1-hour increments, via the official SUISEI LINE account. On-site payment only on the day of use.

Hours & Rates

Open 6:00–23:00 daily. General rate: 1,000 JPY per hour. Student rate: 500 JPY per hour (valid 6:00–16:00 with school name registered at booking).

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation is free up to 7 days before. 50% of the rental fee applies between 3–6 days before; 100% applies on the day, the day before, or two days before. Cancellation fees must be transferred to our designated bank account within 1 week (transfer fees borne by the customer). A late fee of 200% applies if not paid within 1 week.

Important Rules

Indoor shoes only (bare feet or socks are fine; outdoor shoes are not allowed). Playing instruments and band practice (including vocals) are not allowed due to soundproofing and electrical constraints. Smoking is not allowed inside or around the building. Please clean the floor before leaving — the studio operates on a self-cleaning policy.

Full Terms (Japanese)

The complete terms of use, member agreement, and detailed prohibited items are provided in Japanese only. For English support or questions, please contact us via the official LINE account or Instagram before booking.
