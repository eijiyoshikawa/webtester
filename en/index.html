STUDIO

STUDIO

Renovated in March 2026. In addition to dance, the studio is suitable for yoga, aerobics, Zumba, and other multipurpose use. (Approx. 31 m²)

02

SPEAKER

High-quality JBL speakers are available. Connect via AUX cable or wirelessly via Bluetooth. (CDs are not supported)

03

MONITOR

A large display lets you review each movement, making the studio perfect for beginners or for learning choreography step by step.

04

LIGHTING

Two types of lighting are available — ideal for filming social media content or memorable videos. Colors can be adjusted to your liking.
